Privacy, in plain words

Short version: your card choices and games stay on your device. We do not use accounts, analytics, ads, or trackers.

What the site handles

Your current recipe exists only in the open browser tab. It disappears when you close or reload the page. When you download a game, your browser saves that file where you choose.

Offline use

A service worker may cache public site files on your device so the workshop can work offline. This cache contains the app itself—not your choices or personal information. You can remove it by clearing site data in your browser.

Children

We do not ask for names, ages, contact details, or any other personal information. An adult and child can use the tool without creating an account.

Questions

This open-source project is maintained by the Param Factory. Review or report issues through the project repository.
